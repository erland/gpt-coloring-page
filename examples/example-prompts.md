# Exempel på färdiga bildprompts

## Drakmotiv, 6 år, referens + målarbild
Create a printable A4 portrait coloring page for a 6-year-old child. Layout: small fully colored reference illustration at the top filling maximum 25% of the page height, and a large black-and-white outline coloring version of the same subject below. Subject: a friendly fire dragon with a warm orange body, cream-colored belly, rounded snout, kind expressive eyes, two small curved horns, broad bat-like wings, sturdy legs, short arms with rounded claws, and a long tail ending in a glowing ember-shaped flame. Make the creature brave, playful, and majestic, standing on a simple rocky hill with a few smoke swirls in the background. The lower coloring image should have thick clean outlines, white fill areas, no shading, no gray tones, and large child-friendly shapes. Keep the page uncluttered.

## Dinosaurie, 4 år, endast målarbild
Create a printable A4 portrait coloring page for a 4-year-old child. One large black-and-white outline illustration of a friendly baby dinosaur with a round body, big happy eyes, short legs, tiny arms, and a smiling face. Use thick clean outlines, very large white fill areas, no shading, no gray tones, no color, and no complex background. Keep the page uncluttered and child-friendly.

## Tårta, 7 år, referens + målarbild
Create a printable A4 portrait coloring page for a 7-year-old child. Layout: small fully colored reference illustration at the top filling maximum 25% of the page height, and a large black-and-white outline coloring version of the same subject below. Subject: a cheerful three-layer birthday cake with candles, frosting drops, berries, sprinkles, and a smiling decorative cake topper. The lower coloring image should have thick clean outlines, white fill areas, no shading, no gray tones, and large child-friendly shapes. Keep the page uncluttered.

## Hus, 5 år, endast målarbild
Create a printable A4 portrait coloring page for a 5-year-old child. One large black-and-white outline illustration of a cozy little house with a round door, two windows, a chimney, flowers beside the path, and a smiling sun in the sky. Use thick clean outlines, large white fill areas, no shading, no gray tones, and simple child-friendly shapes. Keep the page uncluttered.
